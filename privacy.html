Privacy Policy: Ian's Rocket Uploader

Ian's Rocket Uploader is a private tool used by IQopolis LLC to upload Ian's Rocket videos to IQopolis's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

  • It is used only by IQopolis, on IQopolis's own Google account. It does not offer sign-in to anyone else.
  • It uses Google account access only to upload videos to, and read the upload status of, IQopolis's own YouTube channel.
  • It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. The only credential it keeps is IQopolis's own sign-in token, stored privately on IQopolis's computers.
  • Its use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
